Crystal notes

The crystal has six unique vertices and eight triangular faces. The indexed geometry reuses the six vertices and averages the normals at shared corners, giving the crystal smooth highlights. Press F to compare it with the faceted version, which uses 24 position entries (three per face) so each triangle has its own normal. With the default front-side material, reversing two indices in a triangle flips the face inward and makes it disappear when viewed from outside. The submitted geometry uses outward-facing winding on all eight triangles.
